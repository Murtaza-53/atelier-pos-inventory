# Customer Due & Khata Dashboard

## Scope
Replace the current inventory view at `/` with the supplied Customer Due & Khata dashboard while preserving the Atelier Retail shell and visual system.

## Build
- Update the sidebar so Customers (CRM) is active and the customer-management callout matches the reference.
- Recreate the page header, four actions, five customer/due summary cards, due-age donut, six-month trend chart, and quick actions.
- Add the ten visible customer rows with segment filters, search, overdue states, pagination, and row selection.
- Add the selected customer’s khata details, summary tabs, transaction history, recent payments, top due customers, and reminder panel.
- Keep the layout usable on smaller screens with horizontal table access and stacked detail panels.

## Interactions
- Search filters customers by name, phone, khata number, and segment.
- Segment and group selectors filter the customer list.
- Selecting a customer updates the details panel.
- Detail tabs, pagination, reminder, add-customer, export, new-khata, and quick-action controls respond visibly.
